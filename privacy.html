Privacy policy

Publisher: RDanielW.

Summary

Local Page Exporter captures pages selected by you and processes and stores those captures on your computer. The extension does not send captures, browsing data, or telemetry to the publisher or third parties. This release has no accounts, analytics, advertising, payment processing, or contribution link.

Information accessed and stored locally

When you click the toolbar icon and request capture, the extension accesses that page's visible pixels and available text, links, and images. It stores screenshot sections, sanitized readable text/HTML, original-image snapshots where browser access permits, page title and URL, browser zoom, capture date, and capture/progress metadata in this extension's local IndexedDB database.

Pages can contain personal or confidential information, including visible names, communications, financial/health details, or form fields. A screenshot can include such information even where the readable export omits forms. Capture only content you intend to retain.

Purpose and permissions

Local data is used only to create and manage your requested captures, previews, and exported files. The activeTab permission grants temporary access to the selected tab. The scripting permission runs bundled capture functions there. The downloads permission saves requested PNG/PDF files and checks the status of those downloads. No separate browsing-history, cookies, credential, or debugger permission is requested.

No publisher collection, tracking, or sale

The extension does not transmit captured content off-device to the publisher or another recipient. It does not monitor background browsing history, sell or share captures, use them for advertising/profiling, collect analytics or crash reports, or load remote scripts/fonts in its own interface. No publisher server receives your captures.

Normal source-website requests may occur as the page loads or scrolls. Following a link in an exported document, selecting a network printer, saving to a synchronized folder, or using device backups involves your chosen services and their own settings. The extension does not control those services.

Retention, security, and deletion

Captures remain locally until you delete them, the browser clears/evicts the data, or you uninstall the extension. Storage has no fixed retention period and is not guaranteed permanent. It is not encrypted separately by the extension and depends on your device/browser-profile protections. Anyone with access to your browser profile or exported files may be able to access the content.

Use a capture's trash button to delete its stored session, screenshot tiles, readable content, and image snapshots. Uninstalling removes extension storage through the browser. Downloaded files remain until you delete them separately. Keep your own exports of important records.

Support communications and this website

If you voluntarily contact support, the publisher receives the information you send, such as your email address and issue description, and uses it to respond. Do not send private captures or URLs unless you intend to disclose them. Support correspondence is separate from the extension's capture storage; contact the publisher about deletion of correspondence.

These page files contain no analytics, scripts that set cookies, external fonts, payment integration, or trackers. The website is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website does not receive captures from the extension.

The support mailbox uses Outlook.com. Microsoft processes email and account-service data according to the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Emailing support deliberately shares your message and sender information; capture data is never attached or sent automatically.

Changes and contact

Future changes to capture data handling or an optional contribution service will be reflected in an updated policy and relevant store disclosures. Questions about this policy: rdanielwapps2026@outlook.com.

ChatGPT Local Transcript Exporter

Save the open ChatGPT conversation as a formatted transcript on your computer.

Independent extension by RDanielW. Not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. A ChatGPT account and access to the source conversation are required.

Export formats

HTML, direct PDF, native Word (.docx), Markdown, RTF, and JSON. Speaker labels distinguish your messages from ChatGPT responses. Word includes a repeating title header after page one and page-of-total fields. HTML/PDF and Word can include available response images. RTF is a simpler text export.

Capture controls

Open a conversation, select Export Chat, and choose Scan Full Chat. Keep the tab active while the scanner scrolls toward both boundaries. There is no fixed 100-message cap. Capture Visible collects loaded messages, not just viewport pixels. Recapture after changing the response-image or reference checkboxes.

Local processing and network requests

No capture files, conversation text, analytics, or telemetry are uploaded to the publisher. Captures are held in the current tab's memory, without a persistent extension database. Refreshing or closing the tab clears that memory; exported files remain separately.

ChatGPT can request older messages during scrolling. Embedding images may request their original URLs with your browser session. Small citation icons and failed embeds can remain remote references in exported HTML/PDF. Opening those files or following links may contact the source sites. See the product-specific privacy policy.

PDF behavior and limitations

Direct PDF uses Edge's debugger permission only on a temporary renderer tab; a debugging banner is expected. It detaches and closes the tab after rendering, then opens Save As. Very large jobs can hit browser limits; export HTML and use Print → Save as PDF instead.

Exports depend on what ChatGPT supplies in the loaded conversation branch. Hidden branches, unavailable history, collapsed content, remote canvases, original uploads, audio, and video may be absent. Check important records against the source. Transcripts are not exact screenshots or certified evidence; the JSON hash is not proof of origin.

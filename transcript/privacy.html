Privacy policy

Applies to ChatGPT Local Transcript Exporter 0.16.6. Updated November 10, 2026. Publisher: RDanielW. Contact: rdanielwapps2026@outlook.com.

Summary

This independent extension creates local exports of the ChatGPT conversation you select. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. It has no capture server, capture uploads, analytics, advertising, payment processing, or publisher account system.

Information accessed

The extension installs export controls automatically on chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com. When you request capture, it reads available conversation text/HTML, speaker labels, upload references, response image sources/data, conversation title/URL, capture date, and progress metadata. JSON includes a comparison hash of captured content. It does not download original uploaded files, record audio/video, retrieve passwords, or independently query your account's entire history.

Conversations may contain personal or confidential communications, names, attachments, health/financial details, or other sensitive information. Capture only records you intend to retain and are authorized to share.

Local memory and exported files

Captured messages and image cache data are held in the current ChatGPT tab's memory. No persistent localStorage, IndexedDB, or cloud-sync capture database is used. A new full scan resets captured messages; image cache data remains until tab refresh/close. Refreshing or closing the tab clears capture memory. Exported files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them separately. The browser may keep its own caches, history, and download records under its settings.

The extension does not encrypt capture memory or exported files separately. Access depends on your device, browser-profile, and file protections. A comparison hash is not a digital signature or proof of origin. Keep backups of important files and review exports before sharing.

Network activity

No conversation text, capture files, browsing telemetry, or analytics are uploaded to the publisher or a capture service. Scanning can cause ChatGPT's normal requests for older conversation content. When embedding images, the extension may request image URLs already referenced by the conversation, using the browser's current session. The background worker is limited to HTTPS requests to the supported ChatGPT sites and OpenAI image CDN hosts oaiusercontent.com and oaistatic.com.

Small citation icons or images that cannot be embedded can remain remote references in HTML/PDF. Opening an export or following its links may contact those source sites. Such requests can expose your IP address and normal request/session information to the source host. This extension does not promise zero network activity or that every exported file is fully self-contained. Image checkboxes change capture behavior; recapture after changing them.

The extension loads no remote executable code and has no analytics or crash-report uploads. Synchronized folders, network/cloud printers, third-party viewers, and backups follow your own settings and those services' policies. ChatGPT's own service and account data remain subject to OpenAI's policies.

Permission purposes

Site access: add the capture interface and read/scroll rendered messages on the two ChatGPT sites; retrieve accessible response images from the listed OpenAI image hosts. No all-site or blanket *.openai.com permission is requested.

debugger: attach only to a temporary local render tab created for direct PDF export; render and read PDF data, then detach and close that tab. Edge displays a debugging banner during the job. It is not used to inspect unrelated tabs or the source ChatGPT tab.

downloads: save the PDF you request and look up its own download result. Other formats use local Blob downloads. There is no cookies API, background browsing-history collection, or credential collection.

Your controls

Use Stop Scan to interrupt scanning. Recapture with your preferred image settings. Refresh/close the tab to clear captures and image cache memory. Delete exported files through your file manager. Disable or uninstall the extension to remove its controls/site access. Uninstalling does not delete previously exported files. No publisher capture database exists from which records must be deleted.

Support and this website

If you voluntarily email support, the publisher receives and uses your email address and the information you send to respond. Do not send confidential transcripts, URLs, account details, or images unless you intend to disclose them. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the support address. Outlook.com processes email under Microsoft's applicable policies.

This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It includes no analytics, remote fonts, advertising, payment integration, or scripts setting cookies. GitHub Pages may log visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site receives no captures from the extension.

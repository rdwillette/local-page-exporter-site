Support

ChatGPT Local Transcript Exporter is an independent extension by RDanielW, not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.

Email rdanielwapps2026@outlook.com with the extension version, Edge version, steps to reproduce, and error text. Avoid private conversation text, account details, URLs, and images unless you intend to share them.

Scan incomplete?

Keep the chat tab active, review the message count and partial warning, and compare with the original. ChatGPT can change its rendered layout or withhold older/hidden content. The extension cannot promise complete capture of every conversation.

PDF too large?

Direct PDF has browser rendering/download limits. Save HTML, open it in Edge, and use Print → Save as PDF. The temporary debugging banner is expected during direct PDF export.

Word page numbers?

Open print preview or update Word fields. Native page-number fields may initially show cached values.

Missing images?

Enable response-image embedding and recapture. Authentication, source-site restrictions, or expired URLs can prevent embedding. Citation icons and failed embeds can remain remote references. Original uploads, audio, and video are not archived as media files.
